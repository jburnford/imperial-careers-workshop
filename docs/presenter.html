The live lookup

Three searches, in this order, with the statements read aloud for the first two: Trimen (supported), Price (supported, and the gate had rejected it), Tibbits (nothing; record the search). If the endpoint is down, the same three are written up on the Ground page.

The control

Jim’s Gemini 3.8 Flash run of 7 October is scored in outputs/ground-test/gemini-scoring.md: zero of sixteen person identifiers, six not even people, seven near-miss numbers. If running a chatbot live, use entry 4 (Archer) or 18 (Marsh): both produced a near-miss number, which makes the point better than a wild one.

Online participation

  • Remote participants see the same pages and both exercises. Nothing requires a login.
  • Both exercise pages have a Copy summary button that produces one line for the Zoom chat, so the chat monitor can read decisions out.
  • The decision exercise can Download TSV with reasons. If the organizers want them collected, say where to send the file.
  • The atlas at jimclifford.ca/col_matching works in any browser; invite remote participants to search a name while the room marks up entries.

What not to claim

  • The audit numbers are from one 186-record sample of the structuring stage; they are not an accuracy rate for the whole graph.
  • The silver standard measured the deterministic tier; the model judge’s agreement rates were still pending in September.
  • The published counts in the col_matching README, stats file and atlas disagreed in September; the numbers on these pages are from kg_stats.json and the atlas meta.json of 4 September 2026, copied into outputs/atlas/.
  • The Gemini control is one run of one model with one prompt. It shows the failure mode; it does not rank models.
