The correction replay

python3 scripts/demo_ditto_fix.py

This needs neither a model nor a network. It checks three saved records and twelve events, changes Balmer’s literal “do.”, preserves the other fields, and checks that a second run makes no further changes. Constructed cases check that a missing predecessor stays unresolved. The saved report includes before, after and the reason.

Call this a workshop replay. It is not a production rerun or a measurement of the graph’s post-fix accuracy. Blakely and Connolly’s unrelated errors remain in the replay output.

Two unfinished repairs worth showing

Wodehouse: the saved atlas still has the Venezuela mission and the 1861 high commissionership in the Union of South Africa, Belmopan as the 1851 seat of Honduras, and Honduras inheriting British Guiana in the India Office record. The 1861 error followed a blanket South Africa cache patch. Say these are open defects.

Brewster: correcting Victoria’s geography leaves the saved graph asserting a 1912–1916 appointment from a clause saying he was defeated. Ask for a verdict before revealing it. Keep the defeat as source evidence; do not treat it as an office held. This is also an open defect in the saved graph.

The live lookup and saved runs

Search Trimen, Price, then Tibbits. Read the retrieved evidence before deciding. Price is a useful pause: a rare name and compatible career context support the match, but another source would strengthen it. Tibbits is unresolved after the search, not proof that no item exists. The Ground page and answer key are the fallback.

Use the saved chatbot runs. Gemini supplied sixteen person identifiers, none correct; Opus and Astra supplied sixteen accepted subject matches each. Opus’s first pass stopped before places; its second reached seventeen. The place counts use different units and coverage. These are examples of workflows, not a controlled model ranking. Keep Harris’s particular date conflict for the final exercise.

The cost slide distinguishes the coding agent, corpus reader and hard-case reviewer. Twenty-seven minutes for twenty entries extrapolates to about twenty-five days serially at that observed rate. Caching, concurrency and task changes affect it; it is not an invoice or production forecast. Participants without a GPU can begin with twenty records and a hosted model.

Participation and debrief

Use the same prompts in the room and on Zoom. Pause before the Abery decision and the Brewster second reveal. Ask for evidence, not a show of confidence. No polling software is required.

For the final exercise, the page puts R1, R3 and R8 first; the other five are further practice. Select R8 beside Copy decision and reason for a chat response. Paper users have the same proposals and evidence. The TSV download retains all reasons and the case-set fingerprint; do not promise collection unless a destination has been arranged.

For Harris, invite both an “accept with conflict recorded” and an “unresolved pending a check” response. The key distinction is between accepting the identity and replacing the birth year in the source. Check the scan, another edition and the external date’s cited source. Repeated copies of an account do not make independent corroboration.

Claims to keep bounded

  • Roster counts are sums of within-List person records, not unique people across both services. The 186 bridges are proposed links; a small September check supported 11 of 15.
  • The structuring audit has three model-reviewed samples, before normalisation. Round 1 has eight major findings on six records. The samples do not establish finished-graph accuracy.
  • A labelled sample measures the population and decision type it was designed for. The 76% figure concerns “different” decisions in a particular risky stratum, not overall recall.
  • An identifier resolving to the expected label is not enough to establish a historically correct match.
  • Administrative seats are plotting proxies. Arcs connect recorded postings; they are not observed journeys.
  • The published Imperial Careers site is unchanged by this workshop revision.
