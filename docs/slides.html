What is OCR? Optical character recognition turns a page image into text. These volumes were read by olmOCR, an open model that returns text blocks with their position on the page. OCR errors and printers’ errors look alike in the text; the scan decides.

What is “do.”? Ditto: “the same as before”. The printer saved space by pointing back to an earlier place or office. A reader resolves it without thinking; a program has to be told.

What is a regular expression? A pattern that matches text by its shape. 1[89]\d\d finds every year from 1800 to 1999 in an entry. It cannot say which posting the year belongs to, or that “do.” points back three clauses.

  1. This is a source problem historians know well: recurring headings, names, dates and offices without a uniform sentence structure. OCR can add variation, but even perfectly transcribed entries contain elision, abbreviations, compound clauses and concurrent appointments. Regular expressions recognise specified patterns; increasingly elaborate rules did not cover the collection reliably. Python is still useful for segmentation, regular cases and checks. Explain the three rows: Balmer inherits the preceding service’s place; Blakely’s “B. Honduras” is British Honduras and April 1898 dates his clerkship; Trimen’s lecturer and British Museum posts overlap. The third example is in the saved Trimen source pack. The conclusion of our discussion was that Python rules alone were insufficient for this corpus. The hard part is binding the right position, place and date, not simply detecting a number.

What is Qwen? A family of open-weight language models from Alibaba’s Qwen team. Open weight means the trained model can be downloaded and run on your own hardware, or reached through a hosted service. The merge rules, in brief Merge only on positive evidence: an overlapping dated event, a shared honour with its year, near-identical education, or an OCR variant with a consistent career. Birth year alone never splits: it is often misread. Different spelled forenames keep people apart.

What is vector search? Each Wikidata item is stored as a list of numbers that captures what its description means, so a query finds items close in meaning, not only in spelling. It still returns candidates, not answers: on 11 October “Penang Straits Settlements” put the whole colony first, the modern Malaysian state eighth and Penang Island 24th. What are SPARQL and QLever? SPARQL is the query language for Wikidata: ask for every item matching a pattern of statements, such as “held this office and was born in…”. QLever, from the University of Freiburg, is a fast public engine that runs such queries over all of Wikidata.

32–33. Use QLever when the entry gives enough structured evidence to ask an exact question: an office, a jurisdiction and a birth year. This is a real result: the project’s query for Northern Rhodesia governorships returned these seven people. Two were born in 1889, so the birth year alone cannot decide; name, birth year and office together do. Rule (kg_ground_persons_l2_match.py): equal surname, compatible given names, no link if birth years differ by more than two, exactly one surviving holder, and a guard against two people claiming one QID. Then verify_person_deaths.py drops any match where the List shows the person more than a year after Wikidata’s death date (21 dropped).
