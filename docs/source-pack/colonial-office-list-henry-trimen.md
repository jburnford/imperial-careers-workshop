## 1883

TRIMEN, HENRY, M.B. (Lond.), F.L.S.—Formerly lecturer on botany at St. Mary's Hospital, London, 1867 to 1875, was senior assistant to department of botany, British Museum, 1869 to 1879; director of the Royal Botanical Gardens, Ceylon, Feb., 1880; author of several works and papers.
