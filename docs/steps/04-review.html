Clean, together

Minutes 30–45. Is this what the page says? Then: what the correction becomes.

The reading

The OCR for this corpus is Chandra 2, a vision-language model. It was tested on the collection and performs well. Here is what it wrote for the last paragraphs of Agar’s letter, and what the page says:

Chandra 2 The page
… and were now in a full condition and in flower. … and were growing in a bad situation and not robust.
I have by my prints of the leaves of some of the trees as well as the other that Mr. Moens had sent to be true Ledgerianas … I have many plants from the seed of the specimen tree as well as the others that Mr. Moens declared to be true Ledgerianas …

Open the scan, right-hand column, last three paragraphs. The three readings side by side, including the old archive.org text layer, are in the source pack.

Three things to say about this in the room:

  • The page is clean and easy to read. This is not a recognition failure. The language model inside the OCR model wrote a different sentence, and because it is a language model, the sentence reads well.
  • The old OCR got it right, noisily. “ami were growing; in a bad situation and not robust.” Its errors look like errors. The new OCR’s errors read like prose. OCR is better than it was, and not perfect.
  • The frontier models noticed and could not fix it; the affordable model did not notice. Runs A and B flagged these sentences as uncertain and guessed (“in full bloom”, “the others that Moens had said”); every guess was wrong. Run C, the Qwen model the project uses at scale, reported “in a full condition and in flower” as fact. A model may tell you a sentence reads oddly. Only the page tells you what it says.

The decision

Reject the reading. Record the corrected transcription with the scan as its source. Keep the OCR as delivered beside it, so the two can be compared, and so the record of what the model saw is honest.

That is case R3 in the exercise.

What the correction becomes

One corrected sentence is worth little. The reason for the correction is worth a lot, because it tells the model what to look for across the collection. The prompt:

This example is wrong for the following historical reason: [explain]. Find other records that might have the same problem. Propose a correction and show which records it would affect. After we review the change, rerun a small batch and show both corrected cases and previously accepted cases that changed.

From this page, three rules follow:

  1. An uncertain reading is not data until someone has looked at the page.
  2. A model’s guess at the intended wording is not a reading.
  3. Where two OCRs disagree on a sentence, send it to a human. The archive.org text layer and Chandra disagree exactly where Chandra is wrong, so a cheap comparison would flag these places across 63,000 pages without re-OCR.

Two larger examples of a correction becoming a rule, from the Tropical pipeline, are in What a correction becomes. They are for afterwards.
