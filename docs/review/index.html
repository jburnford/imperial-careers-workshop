Review exercise: seven decisions from Imperial Careers

AI and History Conference, 15 October 2026. Seven decisions from the Imperial Careers pipeline (the Colonial Office List as structured data): two about structuring a record, two about whether two entries are one person, two about grounding a place, and an extra about grounding a person. Each is a real case from the project's audits, with the evidence the historian had. Your answers stay in this browser until you download or copy them; nothing is sent anywhere.

For each case: read the passage, look at what the model or the pipeline proposed, weigh the evidence, and record a decision with a reason. "Unresolved" is a legitimate answer when the evidence does not settle it; say what would.

What the columns mean

Accept: the proposal is supported by the evidence shown, for this mention. Reject: it is contradicted, or it changes the source's meaning. Unresolved: the evidence does not decide it; write what would. A decision is for this record only: accepting that one "Victoria" is British Columbia does not decide the next one.
